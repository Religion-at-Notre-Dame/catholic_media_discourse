What can be tested

Spanish is higher than English on television and YouTube

Two-sided Fisher exact tests on the misunderstanding flag, within one outlet. Television: Spanish 3.53% (98 of 2,773) versus English 2.06% (105 of 5,101), odds ratio 1.74, p = 0.00013. YouTube: Spanish 6.62% (47 of 710) versus English 2.06% (112 of 5,438), odds ratio 3.37, p ≈ 10⁻¹⁰. Radio: 0.82% versus 0.42%, not significant.

News in this set is 46,583 English excerpts, 46 Spanish, and 3 Portuguese, so a language contrast inside news is empty. Portuguese overall is about 10 excerpts. That is not a test.
